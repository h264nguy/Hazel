About Hazel

The kind of person who shows up for everyone.

Hoài Ngân Phan is bright, energetic, and always ready to make a moment more fun. She is usually the one encouraging everyone to come along, reminding the group what is happening, and making sure nobody misses an event. One of the things I admire most about her is how naturally she helps people when they are having a hard time — even when she sometimes forgets to put herself first.

She can be a little mean sometimes, but that teasing and straightforward side is also part of what makes her feel so genuine. She says what she thinks, keeps things interesting, and somehow that little bit of meanness becomes part of her charm. Underneath it all, she is thoughtful, caring, and willing to be there when it really matters.

Small Message

For Hazel

Always try new things, even if they seem small. You never know which little choice will end up changing your life. That is kind of how we started too — something small slowly became something bigger, something meaningful, and something I am genuinely grateful for. So do not be scared to try, explore, fail, or begin again. I will always be there for you and cheer you on.

Set goals that make you proud, chase the things that matter to you, and believe in yourself a little more every day. You already have a beautiful heart, both inside and out, and the best version of you is still growing.
